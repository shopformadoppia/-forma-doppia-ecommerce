Delivery

Information about order delivery and shipping arrangements.

How Delivery Works

  1. Place your order through our website.
  2. Wait for order confirmation and shipping details.
  3. Our team will coordinate the available delivery arrangement.
  4. Track or follow up on your order using the details provided by the courier, when available.
Delivery fees and estimated arrival times depend on the destination, courier, parcel details, and availability. Final delivery arrangements will be confirmed with the customer.

Courier Partners

These courier brands are displayed as possible delivery options. Actual availability will be confirmed when the order is processed.

Delivery Reminders

  • Provide a complete and accurate delivery address.
  • Keep your phone number available for delivery coordination.
  • Check your order upon receiving it.
  • Contact Customer Care if your order arrives damaged or incorrect.
