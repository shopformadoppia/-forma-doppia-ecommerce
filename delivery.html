How Delivery Works

  1. Place Your Order. Submit your order through our website and provide the necessary delivery information.
  2. Order Confirmation. Wait for confirmation and verification of your order details.
  3. Order Preparation. Your product will be prepared and packed for shipment.
  4. Order Dispatch. Once handed to the courier, shipping information may be provided to the customer.
  5. Delivery. Receive your order at the delivery address you provided.

Order Tracking

If a tracking number is available, customers may use it through the selected courier's official tracking service to check their shipment status.

  • Wait for your order to be dispatched.
  • Check the tracking details provided by the seller or courier.
  • Use the tracking number on the courier's official website, when available.
  • Contact Customer Care if you need assistance with your shipment.
Tracking numbers are not automatically generated by this demonstration website. Tracking details will only be available when provided by the courier.

Possible Delivery Delays

Although we aim to provide timely delivery, some circumstances may affect the estimated arrival of your order.

  • Weekends and public holidays.
  • Severe weather conditions and natural events.
  • Courier operational delays or high shipment volume.
  • Incomplete or incorrect delivery information.
  • Remote or difficult-to-reach delivery locations.

Delivery Reminders

  • Provide a complete and accurate delivery address.
  • Make sure your contact number is active and reachable.
  • Keep your phone available for delivery coordination.
  • Check your order upon receiving it.
  • Contact Customer Care if your order arrives damaged or incorrect.
