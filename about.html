Our Story

Forma Doppia is a furniture concept under JNHJ Furnishings Co. The name combines the idea of form and dual functionality, reflecting our interest in practical furniture that serves more than one purpose.

Our featured concept is a convertible chair-ladder design that brings together seating and step-up functionality in one furniture piece.

We aim to offer functional designs that make everyday spaces more convenient while maintaining a clean and appealing appearance.

Our Purpose

To introduce practical furniture solutions that combine functionality, comfort, and thoughtful design.
