Our Story

Two functions. One thoughtful design.

The name “Forma Doppia” is inspired by the Italian words “forma,” meaning “form,” and “doppia,” meaning “double.” It reflects our product’s unique design, combining functionality and modern aesthetics.

Created by JNHJ Furnishings Co., Forma Doppia brings style, comfort, and practicality into everyday living spaces. Inspired by contemporary furniture and minimalist interiors, it offers a versatile furniture solution designed to complement different lifestyles.
