Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Forma Doppia.

What is Forma Doppia?

Forma Doppia is a furniture concept under JNHJ Furnishings Co., featuring practical furniture designs such as a convertible chair-ladder.

How can I place an order?

Visit the Shop Now page, select a product, add it to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You will need to sign in or create an account to submit an order.

What payment methods are available?

Available options may include GCash, Maya, and Cash on Delivery. Payment availability will be confirmed during order processing.

How much is the delivery fee?

Delivery fees depend on your location, parcel details, and courier availability. The final arrangement will be confirmed with you.

How can I track my order?

Once your order is processed and handed to a courier, tracking information may be provided when available.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Please contact Customer Care as soon as possible. Changes or cancellations depend on the current status of your order.

What should I do if my order arrives damaged?

Contact us and provide your order details and clear photos of the concern so we can review your request.

How can I ask about wholesale orders?

Send your inquiry to jnhjfurnishingscompany@gmail.com for wholesale availability and pricing information.

Still have questions?

We're happy to help with your inquiries.
